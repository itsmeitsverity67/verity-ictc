About us

A small, fictional outfitter for archers and outdoor lovers.

Fletch & Fern Outfitters is an imaginary store created for a school web-development project. Our pretend shop helps new archers find safe, well-explained gear and gives experienced archers a tidy place to restock arrows, targets and trail equipment.

The name comes from fletching – the feathers or vanes that steady an arrow – and the ferns that grow along forest trails.

Beginner friendlyPlain-language descriptions for every product.
Safety firstWe encourage lessons at a local club before shooting.
Outdoors for allGear for backyards, ranges and woodland trails.
